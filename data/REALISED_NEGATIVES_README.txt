ReaLISED detector negatives: speech, television, water, vacuum cleaner.
Source labels were used; each clip has not been manually auditioned for incidental impacts.
Counts match manual positive recordings per split. Duration range 1.1-3.5 s; nearest duration selection.
No synthetic audio, repetition, padding or gain normalization; mono PCM16 WAV 16 kHz.
Consecutive source ID blocks: first 70% train, next 15% val, remainder test.
Capture-session and speaker IDs are unavailable. Splits are NOT proven independent.
Manual positive recordings and ReaLISED negatives have different microphones/environments; source shortcuts may inflate validation results.
